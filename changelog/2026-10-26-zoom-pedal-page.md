Beta: a Zoom MultiStomp gets its pedal page, the Octatrack page for the pedal (`#zoom-ms-70cdr/pedal`, beta testers only). Over Web MIDI in Chrome or Edge it shows the pedal's model and firmware, lists what's on it and backs up every file into a zip. It writes nothing to the pedal yet. Visitors without the beta who open the address go to the machine's library. Its Setup page compares the firmware routes: Zoom's firmware with added effects (no reflash), MIDISTOMP 2.0, and a fully custom firmware in development. Each is flashed with its own updater, never by the site.
