Beta: a Zoom MultiStomp gets its pedal page, the Octatrack page for the pedal (`#zoom-ms-70cdr/pedal`, beta testers only). Over Web MIDI in Chrome or Edge it shows the pedal's model and firmware, lists what's on it and backs up every file into a zip. It writes nothing to the pedal yet. Visitors without the beta who open the address go to the machine's library. Its Setup page shows the two routes, as on the Octatrack: effect files on Zoom's firmware (no reflash, works today), and MIDISTOMP as the base underneath, to be built and flashed through Modwerk later. The page doesn't flash firmware yet. The Zoom docs follow the owner's plan: MIDISTOMP becomes the base and the effects its modules, with Zoom's firmware and effect files as the route without a base; the module contract's install now describes the USB file install, and its ID ledger records GRDLOOP (497).
